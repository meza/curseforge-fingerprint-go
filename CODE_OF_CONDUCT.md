# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and respectful
community for everyone who participates.

## Expected Behaviour

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, welcome constructive
feedback, respect differing experiences and viewpoints, and take
responsibility for mistakes. Contributions should support a collaborative
environment focused on the project and its community.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, and
unwelcome sexual attention are not acceptable. Do not publish another
person's private information without explicit permission or engage in conduct
that would reasonably make others feel unsafe or excluded.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, discussions, and other communication channels. It also applies when
someone represents the project in public, such as by using an official project
account or speaking on behalf of the project.

## Report a Concern

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a contact
method listed on their GitHub profile. Include the relevant links, context,
and any supporting evidence. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

## Confidentiality and Safety

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and share
information only when needed to assess or resolve the concern. Immediate
safety takes priority. Anyone facing an urgent threat should contact the
appropriate local emergency or support service.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, limit participation, or ban a
participant when conduct violates this Code of Conduct. Enforcement decisions
will consider the severity, context, and pattern of behaviour. Retaliation
against anyone who raises a concern or assists with an investigation is not
acceptable.
